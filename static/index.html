    Weekly Auction

    Sealed bids: cards resolve rarest first, then alphabetically, and everyone automatically bids $0 on every card. For a card with N copies, all copies trade at one price — the Nth-highest bid, which is $0 whenever fewer than N real bids exist. Real bidders win copies first; leftover copies are handed to random non-bidders for free. Ties are broken randomly. One bid per card; re-bidding replaces your earlier bid. A single bid can't exceed your balance, but there's no cap on your total bids — as you win and spend, your remaining bids are trimmed down to your remaining balance.
